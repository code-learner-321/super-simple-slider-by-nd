# Super Simple Slider By Nd

A lightweight, developer-friendly Gutenberg block slider built for WordPress. `Super Simple Slider By Nd` allows you to effortlessly create and manage image sliders using a dedicated Custom Post Type, providing a clean and intuitive interface directly within the Gutenberg editor.

## Features

- **Custom Post Type Integration:** Manage your slides easily from a dedicated "Slides" menu in your WordPress dashboard.
- **Gutenberg Ready:** Seamlessly insert your sliders into posts and pages using the custom Gutenberg block.
- **Lightweight & Fast:** Optimized for speed, ensuring your sliders load efficiently without unnecessary bloat.
- **Simple Configuration:** No complex settings; just create your slides and publish.

## Installation

### Method 1: WordPress Dashboard (Recommended)

1. Download the `super-simple-slider-by-nd.zip` file.
2. Log in to your WordPress admin panel.
3. Navigate to **Plugins > Add New**.
4. Click **Upload Plugin** at the top.
5. Choose the downloaded `.zip` file and click **Install Now**.
6. Once installed, click **Activate Plugin**.

### Method 2: FTP/SFTP Installation

1. Unzip the downloaded `super-simple-slider-by-nd.zip` file.
2. Connect to your server via FTP/SFTP.
3. Upload the `super-simple-slider-by-nd` folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
4. Log in to your WordPress admin panel.
5. Navigate to **Plugins > Installed Plugins**.
6. Find "Super Simple Slider By Nd" and click **Activate**.

## How to Use

1. **Add Slides:** Go to the new **Slides** menu in your WordPress admin sidebar and click **Add New**. Add a title and featured image for your slide.
2. **Insert Slider:** Open any Post or Page in the Gutenberg editor.
3. Click the **(+) Block Inserter** button.
4. Search for "Super Simple Slider" and add the block to your content.
5. Select the desired slider configuration in the block settings panel and update/publish your post.

## Requirements

- WordPress 5.8 or higher.
- PHP 7.4 or higher.
